About the Festival

The very first Various Artists Festival by Sort of Vain LLC is coming to Lancaster, PA on November 28th at our city’s most exciting new performance venue, West Art.

Sort of Vain is a Lancaster based booking and live production company founded in 2026 by Alex Hart. Our goal as a business is to bring the unique work of Lancaster area artists to a larger audience by hosting events and experiences designed to highlight the incredible talents of Lancaster's creative community.

Various Artists Festival will be a full-day event, across multiple stages, all in one venue - featuring one of a kind performances by local and regional musicians, comedians, and other genre-bending artists.

Our team is dedicated to producing an unforgettable experience that is full of surprises; where artists can come together in mutual appreciation and audiences can discover something new.
