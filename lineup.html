Music headliner: Spilly Cave

  • Played guitar alongside Samia, Blu DeTiger, and Taylor Mackall
  • Self-titled record earned millions of streams in its first year
  • Invited by Lovejoy's Ash Kabosu to open their sold out national tour

After growing up in rural Pennsylvania, Spilly Cave moved to NYC for college where he got the opportunity to play guitar alongside artists such as Samia, Blu DeTiger, and Taylor Mackall. He also supported the writing process and played guitar on Taylor Mackall's record "The Boy and The Saints," which features the legendary artists Sam Wilkes, Sam Gendel, Sean Hurley, and Fabiano de Nascimento. Through all of this though, Spilly Cave was eager to one day create his own music and produce everything himself.

Returning home to Harrisburg, Spilly Cave spent years learning production with the help of local friends. This expertise in live performance and composition led to the genesis of his first self-produced album in 2019.

During the pandemic, he ventured into TikTok creating and released his self-titled record which earned millions of streams in its first year. This all culminated in an invite from Ash Kabosu of the UK indie rock band Lovejoy to open on their sold out tour across the country.

We're extremely grateful that Spilly Cave has decided to join us as our first ever music headliner.

Comedy headliner: Alan Massenburg

  • Finalist in HBO's American Black Film Festival stand-up competition
  • Debut album "We Got Action" reached #1 on the iTunes charts
  • Performed at Harlem's iconic Apollo Theater

Alan Massenburg is a comedian and actor from Philadelphia who's been making some big moves lately. He was a finalist in HBO's American Black Film Festival stand-up competition, has been a guest on The Drew Barrymore Show's Dad Joke segment, appeared on TMZ Live and SiriusXM's "Sway in the Morning," and has even performed at Harlem's iconic Apollo Theater.

Alan's debut comedy album "We Got Action" reached #1 on the iTunes charts, and despite his hate/hate relationship with TikTok, he's amassed over 10 million views and more than 30,000 followers across platforms.

We're thrilled to have Alan as our inaugural Comedy Headliner.
