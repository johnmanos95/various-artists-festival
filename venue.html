West Art

This November, the very first Various Artists Festival takes over West Art, a historic church in the heart of Lancaster, transformed into a one-of-a-kind venue for live music, comedy, and visual art. Part of what makes this day special is the building itself: as you make your way through every room and up every floor, you'll stumble into something new, comedy around one corner, music down the next hall, and a few surprises we're saving for the day of. It's a festival built for wandering and discovery.

Four Stages, One Building

From grand-scale energy to deep, personal basement vibes, Various Artists spreads out across every level of West Art's historic building.
