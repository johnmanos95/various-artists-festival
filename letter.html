About the Festival

A first for Lancaster.

The Various Artists Festival is a brand-new, all-day celebration of local music, comedy, and visual art — built by artists, for the community that inspires them.

Nov 28, 2026 · Lancaster, PA

Dear Neighbors and Community Partners,

The very first Various Artists Festival is coming to Lancaster this fall — and on behalf of the production team and featured artists, I am inviting you to consider being a part of this extraordinary event as a sponsor and community partner.

On November 28th, this unprecedented event will feature a wide variety of local acts across multiple stages for an all-day experience at West Art in Lancaster City. Local music, comedy, and visual artistry of all genres and tastes will be showcased and celebrated throughout the day — and we are excited to offer you the opportunity to support our dedicated team in their goal of producing an event that brings the eclectic work of Lancaster and Pennsylvania based artists to a broader audience.

Ultimately, we aspire to strengthen the relationships between local creatives and elevate our ever-growing community through this event. This ambitious mission will require the patronage and support of local businesses like yours in order to be fulfilled.

November 28th will be an unforgettable day in Lancaster City. Please consider joining us in our efforts to create something special for the community we love.

Sincerely,
Alex Hart
Creative Director

The Venue

West Art

West Art is a historic church building at 816 Buchanan Ave in Lancaster City, reimagined as a multi-story venue for live music, performance, and visual art. Co-founded by Rufus Deakin and Josh Gibbel between 2021 and 2023, it's become a home base for the kind of eclectic, boundary-crossing programming the Various Artists Festival is built around.

On festival day, the entire building comes alive — from the grand sanctuary to the basement — giving sponsors visibility across every level of the experience.

City Hall Stage

Main stage, high-visibility banner placement.

The Sanctuary

The heart of the historic church, reserved for headline moments.

Art Bar

Visual art on display alongside a full bar.

The Elbow Room

Basement-level, close-quarters live music.

The Mission

Strengthen local creatives. Elevate the community.

Everything about this festival — the lineup, the venue, the partnerships we're building — is in service of one goal: bringing the eclectic work of Lancaster and Pennsylvania-based artists to a broader audience, and strengthening the relationships between them.
